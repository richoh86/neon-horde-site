Common questions

How do I move?

Touch anywhere and drag — the stick appears under your thumb. Tap the dash button in the corner (or quick-tap the screen) to blink forward. You cannot be hurt during a dash.

Do my weapons fire by themselves?

Yes. Everything aims and fires on its own. Positioning is the whole game.

What is a fusion?

Take a weapon to level 6 and its partner passive to level 4, and the level-up screen will offer a fusion — a much stronger version of that weapon. There are seven.

How do leaderboards work?

If you are signed in to Game Center, every finished run posts to all three Neon Horde leaderboards — score, kills and time survived — and the trophy button on the main menu opens them. Signing out of Game Center stops it, and nothing else in the game changes.

I lost my progress

Progress lives only on the device, so deleting the app or moving to a new phone without an encrypted backup will clear it. Restoring an encrypted iPhone backup restores it.

The flashing bothers me

Settings → Reduce flashing cuts every full-screen flash and screen shake.

I'm left-handed

Settings → Left-handed layout mirrors the dash button.

How do I restore the Neon Supporter Pack?

Open Settings → Supporter Pack (or the Supporter Pack button on the title screen) and tap Restore Purchases while signed in to the same Apple Account you bought it with. The pack is cosmetic only: it never changes combat, gold or scores. For refunds, use Apple's Report a Problem page.
