PRIVACY

Last updated 26 July 2026

Neon Horde collects nothing. There is no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK, no crash reporter, no account system and no network code in the app at all.

What the app stores

Your progress — best score, longest run, kills, gold and the permanent upgrades you have bought — is written to your device's local application storage (UserDefaults). It never leaves the device. Deleting the app deletes it. You can also erase it yourself from Settings → Reset progress inside the game.

What we receive

Nothing. We have no server, and the app makes no network requests. If you email us for support, we will of course have whatever you choose to put in that email; we use it only to answer you and we do not add you to any list.

Children

The app collects no personal data from anyone, including children under 13. There are no in-app purchases, no advertising and no third-party content.

Third parties

The app bundles no third-party SDKs. Apple may collect standard App Store information when you download or update the app; that is governed by Apple's privacy policy, not ours.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

개인정보 처리방침 (한국어)

네온 호드는 어떤 개인정보도 수집하지 않습니다. 분석 도구, 광고 SDK, 크래시 리포터, 계정 시스템이 전혀 없으며, 앱에 네트워크 통신 코드 자체가 없습니다.

최고 점수·생존 시간·처치 수·골드·영구 강화 등 진행 상황은 기기 내부 저장소에만 기록되며 외부로 전송되지 않습니다. 앱을 삭제하면 함께 삭제되고, 게임 내 설정 → 진행 상황 초기화에서 직접 지울 수도 있습니다. 인앱 결제와 광고, 제3자 콘텐츠는 없습니다.
