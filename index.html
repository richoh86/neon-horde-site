NEON HORDE

Survive eight minutes

You have eight minutes. The horde has no limit. Neon Horde is a one-thumb survivor roguelite for iPhone and iPad. Your weapons aim and fire on their own — your job is to read the arena, keep moving, and pick the right upgrade when the level-up lands.

Dash or die

A real dodge with invulnerability frames. Tap the dash button or flick the screen and blink straight through a wall of enemies.

Fuse your build

Max a weapon and its partner skill and they fuse into something far nastier. Seven fusions to find, and each one changes how the run plays.

Eight minutes, four bosses

A boss every two minutes. Survive to the end and the Nemesis comes for you.

Keep what you earn

Every run pays out gold for the Armory. A bad run still moves you forward.

Built right

No ads No tracking No accounts Plays offline 16 languages

Everything on screen is drawn in code and every sound is synthesised at launch, so the download is a couple of megabytes and the game runs at 120 fps on ProMotion displays.

한국어

주어진 시간은 8분, 몰려오는 적에겐 한계가 없습니다. 네온 호드는 한 손으로 즐기는 생존 로그라이트입니다. 무기는 알아서 조준하고 발사하니, 당신은 전장을 읽고 계속 움직이며 옳은 강화를 고르면 됩니다. 무적 판정이 붙은 대시로 적의 벽을 통과하고, 무기와 스킬을 최대로 올려 일곱 가지 융합 무기를 여세요. 광고도 추적도 계정도 없고, 인터넷 없이 동작합니다.
