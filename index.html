Survivor roguelite

You have eight minutes. The horde has no limit.

A one-thumb survivor roguelite. Your weapons aim and fire on their own — your job is to read the arena, keep moving, and pick the right upgrade when the level-up lands.

iPhone · Free · 120 fps on ProMotion

Fuse your build

Max a weapon and its partner skill and they fuse into something far nastier.

Pulse Bolt plus Power becomes a Railgun that pierces everything on the line. Seven fusions to find, and each one changes how the run plays.

Eight minutes, four bosses

A boss every two minutes. Survive to the end and the Nemesis comes for you.

A full run fits a commute, and losing costs you nothing but time. There is a real dodge with invulnerability frames — tap the button or flick the screen and blink straight through a wall of enemies.

Keep what you earn

A bad run still moves you forward.

Every run pays out gold for the Armory: permanent damage, health, speed, an extra dash charge, even a revive. It applies to every run from then on.

What you get, and what you don’t

No forced ads

The only ads are rewarded videos you choose to watch.

Your choice on tracking

Whether those ads are personalised is your own choice, and saying no costs you nothing.

Plays fully offline

No accounts, no sign-in.

Drawn in code

Everything is drawn in code, so the download stays small.

Dash or die. Most games in this genre never give you an out. This one does.
